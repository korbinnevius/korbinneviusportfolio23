In this sci-fi themed AR experience, the player launches a ball down a rotating tube lined with spinning bricks. Using hand tracking powered by the Ultraleap, the player uses their physical hand movements to destroy the bricks as they approach. When the bricks are cleared, new ones spawn in, creating a challenge that loops until the player is eliminated. This game was inspired by brick break.
